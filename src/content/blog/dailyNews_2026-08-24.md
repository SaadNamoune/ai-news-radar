### [SIMURG: Zero-Leak Real-Time LLM Decoding Corruption Detection](https://github.com/doofzoff/SIMURG)

<small>**Hacker News - Newest: "llm"** &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026-08-24 22:00:25 &nbsp;·&nbsp; `llm-guardrails` `streaming-detection` `corruption-monitoring`</small>

![SIMURG: Zero-Leak Real-Time LLM Decoding Corruption Detection](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/c2adc80a40b9645c87aa3c799f51a76408d1f6e61a5fef1abb408658311ff6c5/doofzoff/SIMURG)

**Overview:** SIMURG is a real-time guardrail system for detecting and aborting LLM decoding corruption mid-stream before it reaches users. It targets statistical anomalies (repetition loops, lexical collapse, script drift) rather than factual hallucinations, enabling immediate retries. **Method:** Uses O(1)-per-character streaming feature extraction (digit fraction, script distribution, compressibility, repetition rate) with a pluggable detector ensemble. Features include conformal calibration for false-alarm control, zero-leak protocol (buffer-and-verify), and integration with vLLM/llama.cpp/Ollama. **Results:** Benchmarks on 243 synthetic streams show 12/21 fully blocked corrupt streams and 0 false alarms on 121 real production texts. Runtime performance (197k chars/sec) far exceeds LLM token generation (250 chars/sec). **Impact:** Advances LLM reliability in production by preventing corrupted outputs from being delivered, with open-source implementation and extensible detector training.

### [BF1: Block-Aligned Dyadic Sparse Attention for Efficient Long-Context Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20427)

<small>**cs.LG updates on arXiv.org** &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026-08-24 05:00:00 &nbsp;·&nbsp; `attention-mechanism` `sparse-attention` `transformers` `long-context-inference`</small>

**Overview:** This paper introduces BF1, a deterministic block-aligned dyadic sparse-attention mechanism designed to reduce the computational cost of dense causal attention in long-context transformers. It targets the O(n²) complexity bottleneck in attention layers while maintaining model performance. **Method:** BF1 combines a small exact local neighborhood, a global first block, and logarithmically spaced historical blocks, achieving O(n log n) selected token interactions per layer and O(log n) graph communication depth. The approach includes a correctness-gated pretrained-model retrofit and systems characterization linking per-layer sparsity to whole-model latency. **Results:** On an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU, BF16 implementation crosses dense attention between 2K–4K tokens, achieving 10.91x per-layer prefill speedup at 32K tokens. Retrofitting 8 of 28 Qwen3-0.6B layers reduces warm time-to-first-token by 7.7%, 11.3%, and 15.3% at 8K, 16K, and 32K, respectively. Under a 1,000-step adaptation protocol, BF1 achieves mean report perplexity of 1.68639, outperforming dense continued training (1.69258) and static-random nonlocal graphs (1.69154). **Impact:** Establishes BF1 as a reproducible sparse operator for long-context transformers, offering a practical path to efficient inference without sacrificing accuracy. Open questions include generalization to larger models and dynamic sparsity adaptation.

[→ Read full article](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20427)

**Overview:** Demonstrates that standard safety alignment in LLMs is superficial, failing to erase harmful intent knowledge and leaving models vulnerable to Semantic Camouflage attacks (e.g., harmful intent wrapped in benign narratives). Proposes Latent Intent Verification (LIV), a lightweight probing defense to detect such attacks. **Method:** Analyzes latent activation trajectories across three SLM families (Phi-3, Qwen2.5, Gemma-2b) and identifies a universal "Intent Horizon" (~15–20% of layers) where harmful intent representations collapse into safe narratives. LIV probes early-layer representations to detect a retained "harm signature." **Results:** On PKU-SafeRLHF, LIV achieves 20–50% higher detection rates than standard guardrails across all tested architectures, neutralizing zero-day semantic attacks without retraining. **Impact:** Advances safety alignment research by exposing a critical architectural weakness and proposing a computationally efficient, architecture-agnostic defense against sophisticated adversarial attacks.
